 \<section style="margin:60px 0;">
 \<h2>Who Is This For\</h2>
 \<div style="padding:25px;background:#fafafa;border-left:5px solid #999;border-radius:8px;">
 \<h3>Every Yid should know Mishnayos by heart\</h3>
 \<p>
 "\<strong>Everyone\</strong> should learn
 \<strong>Mishnayos by heart\</strong>
 according to his capacity,
 \<strong>review\</strong> them while walking in the
 \<strong>street\</strong>,
 and through this we will merit to greet
 \<strong>Moshiach\</strong>."
 \</p>
 \<p>\<em>(Hayom Yom, 21 Kislev)\</em>\</p>
 \</div>
 \</section>
 \<section style="margin:60px 0;">
 \<h2>How it works\</h2>
 \<div style="padding:25px;border:1px solid #ddd;border-radius:12px;">
 \<p>
 Goral on \<strong>Chalokim\</strong> based on the
 \<strong>Rebbe's division\</strong> of
 \<strong>ששה סדרי משנה\</strong>.
 Each Chelek is around 4 Prokim.
 \</p>
 \<ul style="line-height:2;">
 \<li>\<strong>Membership:\</strong> Register at mishna2go.com\</li>
 \<li>\<strong>Quota:\</strong> Select 1, 2, or 3 Mishnayos per week.\</li>
 \<li>\<strong>Delivery:\</strong> Weekly emails and reminders.\</li>
 \<li>\<strong>Learn:\</strong> Memorize the Mishnah/s for the week.\</li>
 \<li>\<strong>Tachlis:\</strong> חזר when you walk in the street.\</li>
 \</ul>
 \</div>
 \</section>
 \<section style="margin:60px 0;">
 \<h2>Overview\</h2>
 \<div style="padding:25px;background:#fafafa;border-radius:12px;">
 \<p>
 \<strong>Chevras Mishnayos Baal Peh\</strong>
 is a great source of
 \<em>bracha\</em> and protection,
 and becoming a member is
 \<strong>completely free.\</strong>
 \</p>
 \<p>
 When you become a member you commit to
 \<strong>memorize 1, 2, or 3 Mishnayos each week\</strong>,
 you're assigned a \<em>Chelek\</em> —
 about \<strong>4 Prokim\</strong> —
 chosen by \<strong>Gorel\</strong>.
 Together, throughout the year we memorize
 \<strong>the entire Mishnayos\</strong>
 concluding
 \<strong>אסרו חג שבועות\</strong>.
 \</p>
 \</div>
